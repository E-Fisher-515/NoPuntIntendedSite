# Constitution of No Punt Intended

This page is the league's official rules document. Scoring, roster size, playoff format, and FAAB settings below are filled from ESPN where the archive has them. Editorial sections should be updated by the commissioner.

## Format & Roster

League size, starter slots, and bench size are defined in ESPN league settings and ingested each season.

## Scoring

The archive stores the ESPN scoring type for each season. Exact point values live in ESPN until they are copied here.

## Waivers

If FAAB is enabled for a season, the acquisition budget is recorded on that season page.

## Trades

Trade deadline and veto rules are governed in ESPN. Record any house rules here.

## Draft

The league drafts offline. ESPN draft boards are not the official record and are not published on this site.

## Playoffs

Playoff team count and regular-season length are ingested from ESPN. Championship, runner-up, and third place are taken from ESPN final standings — not invented.

## Keeper Rules

Add keeper eligibility, round penalties, and max keepers here.

## Tiebreakers

ESPN seeding tiebreakers apply unless the league votes otherwise.

## League Governance

Commissioner: listed in the newsletter config. Amendments should be dated and added to the History timeline.
