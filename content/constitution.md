## 2026 rule changes

- 60-plus yard field goals are now worth 7 points.
- Waivers use FAAB. Standard ESPN FAAB waiver settings apply.
- Bench roster spots: previously 6, now 5. That also means one fewer draft round.
- IR roster spots: previously 1, now 2.

## 2027 buy-in and lottery

The 2027 buy-in will be $100: $75 league buy-in plus $25 for Beast of the Week, paid to the team that scores the most points each regular-season week.

The 2027 draft lottery includes every team. Balls by finish:

- 7th and 8th: 7 balls
- 6th and 9th: 6 balls
- 5th and 10th: 5 balls
- 4th and 11th: 4 balls
- 3rd and 12th: 3 balls
- 2nd and 13th: 2 balls
- 1st and 14th: 1 ball

## Format and roster

Fourteen teams. Starter slots follow the league setting for that season. For 2026: five bench spots and two IR spots.

## Scoring

Point values follow ESPN except where this constitution says otherwise. For 2026, a field goal of 60 yards or more is worth 7 points. Each season's scoring is also listed on that season's page.

## Waivers

FAAB. Standard ESPN FAAB settings apply unless the league votes on a house rule.

## Trades

Trade deadline and veto rules follow ESPN unless the league votes on a house rule.

## Playoffs

Playoff size and regular-season length follow ESPN. Champion, runner-up, and third place come from final standings.

## Keepers

Keeper eligibility, round penalties, and max keepers are set by league vote. To confirm the current keeper rules, contact the commissioner.

## Tiebreakers

ESPN seeding tiebreakers apply unless the league votes otherwise.

## League governance

Amendments should be dated and added here and on the History timeline. The 2026 changes above were published for this season. To add a timeline event, contact the commissioner.
