# Constitution of No Punt Intended

This is the league's official rules document. To propose a change, contact the commissioner.

## Format & Roster

League size, starter slots, and bench size follow the ESPN league settings for each season.

## Scoring

Point values follow ESPN. The scoring format for each year is listed on that season's page.

## Waivers

When FAAB is used, the budget for that season is listed on the season page.

## Trades

Trade deadline and veto rules follow ESPN unless the league votes on a house rule.

## Draft

The league drafts offline. ESPN draft boards are not the official record.

## Playoffs

Playoff size and regular-season length follow ESPN. Champion, runner-up, and third place come from final standings.

## Keeper Rules

Add keeper eligibility, round penalties, and max keepers here.

## Tiebreakers

ESPN seeding tiebreakers apply unless the league votes otherwise.

## League Governance

Amendments should be dated and added to the History timeline. To add a timeline event, contact the commissioner.
