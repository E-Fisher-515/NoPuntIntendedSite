## Draft

Sunday, September 6, around 1pm at Erik's house. Final time TBD.

Buy-in: $75, due to B on or before draft day.

- Food will be provided — Mexican if they are available, otherwise pizza. Be prepared to pitch in about $40 extra to cover food (final amount TBD).
- Optional draft-day prop bet: $5 to bet on a specific player someone in the league will draft. No limit on entries.
- Vote on draft day on whether to add a Rivalry week in week 14. Think who you want as your rival, and why, in case that passes.

## Rule changes

- 60+ yard FG: now worth 7 points.
- FAAB waiver system: standard ESPN FAAB waiver settings apply.
- Bench roster spots: previously 6, now 5. That also means one fewer draft round.
- IR roster spots: previously 1, now 2.

## 2027 buy-in

Next year's buy-in (for the 2027 season) will be $75 for buy-in plus $25 Beast of the Week (paid to the team that scores the most points each regular-season week) = $100.
