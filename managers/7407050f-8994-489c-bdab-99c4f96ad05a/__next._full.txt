## Draft

Sunday, September 6, around 1pm at Erik's house. Final time TBD.

Buy-in: $75, due to B on or before draft day.

- Food will be provided — Mexican if they are available, otherwise pizza. Be prepared to pitch in about $40 extra to cover food (final amount TBD).

- 60+ yard FG: now worth 7 points.
